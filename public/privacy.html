Privacy notice

Effective July 10, 2026

This notice explains how Coach Murray collects and uses information submitted through the website, coaching quiz, verified onboarding, client portal, and coach communication tools.

Information collected

  • Contact details and coaching interests submitted through an application or quiz.
  • Purchase confirmation and subscription status received from Stripe. Coach Murray does not collect or store full card numbers or card security codes.
  • Account identifiers used for secure portal access.
  • Coaching intake details, which may include goals, training history, nutrition preferences, injuries or movement restrictions, lifestyle factors, and an agreement signature.
  • Program activity such as check-ins, progress entries, and messages.
  • Basic technical information needed for security and reliable operation.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, recommend a coaching package, verify purchases, create and deliver coaching services, communicate about the program, maintain account security, and meet legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Marketing messages are sent only when the person has agreed to receive them and may be unsubscribed from at any time.

Service providers

Coach Murray uses service providers to operate the program, including Netlify for website hosting and server functions, Stripe for payment processing, Supabase for authentication and coaching records, and an approved email provider for transactional messages. Each provider receives only the information needed for its role.

Access and retention

Coaching records are limited to the client and authorized coaching staff. Records are retained only as long as needed to deliver services, resolve account or payment issues, meet legal obligations, and maintain appropriate business records. Highly sensitive intake answers are not included in routine notification emails.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your information, subject to any records that must be retained. You may also withdraw marketing consent at any time. To make a request, email jonmurr.fit@gmail.com.

Fitness and health information

Coach Murray provides fitness coaching, not emergency or medical care. Do not use the portal for urgent medical information. Contact an appropriate healthcare professional or emergency service when needed.

Questions or privacy requests

Email jonmurr.fit@gmail.com. Include the email address used with the service so the request can be verified.
