Coaching inquiries

The application asks for your name, email address, fitness goal, preferred start time and coaching interest. Coach Murray uses these details to review your inquiry, contact you and discuss coaching. Please don’t include medical records or other sensitive documents in your initial inquiry.

Website services

This website is hosted by Netlify, which processes coaching applications and client intake submissions and may process technical information for hosting, security and spam filtering. Payment links open Stripe, where payment information is collected and handled under Stripe’s own privacy policy. Card details are not collected by this website’s application form.

The plan finder

Your five quiz answers are used in your browser to suggest a coaching package. The quiz does not require your name or email. If you choose to apply, the selected package can be passed to the application form.

Client onboarding

After enrollment, the client intake asks for information used to develop your coaching plan. The intake describes the additional information requested and the coaching terms. Contact Jonathon if you have questions before submitting.

Questions and requests

To ask about your information, request a correction or request deletion of an inquiry, email jonmurr.fit@gmail.com.
