05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

BEFORE
YOU BEGIN.

Ask Jonathon ↗
Do I need experience in the gym?+

No. Your program is built around your experience, equipment and schedule. I work with beginners as well as experienced lifters and competitors.

How do check-ins work?+

Share your progress and biofeedback through your client portal. Full Coaching includes weekly check-ins, progress photos and coaching feedback within 24–48 hours. Nutrition and Training plans include bi-weekly check-ins.

What if I travel or work long hours?+

Tell me what your week actually looks like. I’ll build around your available time and equipment, with travel-friendly alternatives and adjustments when your schedule changes.

Can I cancel my coaching?+

Yes. Coaching is month-to-month with no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime. Contact me if you need help with your subscription.

Does applying commit me to a plan?+

No. The application starts a conversation. The discovery call is free, and we’ll talk through your goals and the available options before you decide.
